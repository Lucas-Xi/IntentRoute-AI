# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ProxyManager a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn from the experience.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and may remove content or restrict participation when needed.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, available at https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html.
